## Design Context

### Users

People in Linares, Chile exploring chicken and wings for takeaway, delivery, and sharing. The primary tasks are browsing the menu and locating confirmed contact information.

### Brand Personality

Warm, confident, playful. Accessible premium quality without luxury language or aggressive fast-food promotion. Spanish customer copy; English project documentation.

### Aesthetic Direction

Dark editorial food-brand site inspired by Arts District Kitchen. Oversized Space Grotesk Bold and DM Sans, exact charcoal/cream/brasa-red palette, asymmetric media compositions, sparse graphic detail. No stock or generated product photography, invented logo, gradients, rustic textures, or repeated cards.

### Design Principles

- Typography carries the asset-free prototype.
- Alternate proportions and surfaces to create rhythm.
- Make unavailable information honest and visually intentional.
- Preserve keyboard accessibility and reduced-motion behavior.
- Keep every photo slot and the temporary brand mark easy to replace.
